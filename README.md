# About Me:
👋 Hello! I’m a postdoctoral researcher at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. <br>🔬 My work explores the intersection of neuroimaging, movement science, and neuromodulation, with a focus on advancing therapeutic approaches for Parkinson’s disease. <br>🧠 Lately, I’ve been diving into adaptive deep brain stimulation (aDBS) and the ethical dimensions of emerging neurotechnologies. <br>🤝 I’m always interested in connecting with others passionate about neuroscience, machine learning, and innovative ideas.

## 📄 Some publications, see Google Scholar above for full list:
- **Neuroimaging Detectable Differences between Parkinson's Disease Motor Subtypes: A Systematic Review**  
  *Jackson Tyler Boonstra MSc, Stijn Michielse PhD, Yasin Temel MD, PhD, Govert Hoogland PhD, Ali Jahanshahi PhD*  
  *Movement Disorders Clinical Practice, First published: 19 October 2020*  
  [https://doi.org/10.1002/mdc3.13107](https://doi.org/10.1002/mdc3.13107)  
  *Citations: 45*  
  *Summary:* This systematic review summarizes neuroimaging findings distinguishing tremor-dominant, postural instability and gait difficulty, and akinetic-rigid subtypes of Parkinson’s disease, highlighting potential neural markers for earlier diagnosis and personalized treatment.

- **Ethical Imperatives in the Commercialization of Brain-Computer Interfaces**  
  *Jackson Tyler Boonstra*  
  *IBRO Neuroscience Reports, Volume 19, December 2025, Pages 718–724*  
  [https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ibneur.2025.10.004](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ibneur.2025.10.004)
   *Citations: 1*  
  *Summary:* This essay critiques the rapid commercialization of brain-computer interfaces, examining ethical concerns related to safety, data commodification, and governance gaps, and calls for responsible innovation aligned with public values.
